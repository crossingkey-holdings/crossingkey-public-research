# Research Index

This index describes active public research directions. It does not claim that each area has a finished paper or production deployment.

## Human intent and machine execution

Questions include how user intent is represented, how systems surface uncertainty, and how review should work when AI can call tools or change external state.

## Authority-aware automation

Research into the distinction between available capability and granted authority, including scoped permissions, ownership checks, retry, idempotency, and external side effects.

## Verification as interface

Research into making system evidence legible to operators: what was attempted, what completed, what was independently verified, and what remains blocked.

## Productization of technical systems

Research into turning complex AI, API, automation, and infrastructure capability into bounded offers with clear acceptance criteria and handoff.
