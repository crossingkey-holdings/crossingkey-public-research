# Research Methodology

## Source order

Prefer:

1. primary documentation and specifications;
2. direct system behavior;
3. reproducible experiments;
4. high-quality secondary analysis;
5. community reports as signals, not proof.

## Recording results

A research note should state the question, environment, method, observed result, limitations, and conclusion.

## Negative evidence

Failed tests, incompatible assumptions, and disproven hypotheses should be retained when they materially affect the conclusion.

## Safety and privacy

Public research must exclude credentials, private customer information, exploitable operational details, and personal data that is not necessary for the research claim.
